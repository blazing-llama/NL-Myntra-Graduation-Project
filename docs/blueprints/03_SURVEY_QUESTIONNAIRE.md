# Survey Questionnaire — Wishlist Behaviour

**Optional per the brief** — not a required deliverable, but if run, use this. Platform: Google Forms. Target: as many responses as convenient to collect in the Aug 18–24 window; treat as directional colour alongside the discovery engine, not as statistical validation (n will be small — report counts, not percentages, per the project's evidence discipline).

**Design rule followed throughout:** every question maps to a specific hypothesis (H1–H6) or decomposition factor from the blueprint. Nothing is here just to "fill up the data." 11 questions total, mostly single-select, 3–4 minutes to complete.

---

### Screener (disqualifies if failed)

**S1.** In the last 3 months, have you added at least one clothing or fashion item to a wishlist / "saved items" list on Myntra, AJIO, or Nykaa Fashion?
- [ ] Yes
- [ ] No *(disqualify — thank and end)*

**S2.** Which app have you used most for this in the last 3 months?
- [ ] Myntra
- [ ] AJIO
- [ ] Nykaa Fashion
- [ ] More than one, about equally

---

### Core questions

**Q1 — maps to H2 (intent vs. bookmark)**
Thinking about the last item you added to a wishlist, which best describes why you saved it?
- [ ] I plan to buy it soon (within a few weeks)
- [ ] I liked it but wasn't sure yet
- [ ] I was just browsing / getting styling ideas, not planning to buy
- [ ] I was comparing it against other options before deciding
- [ ] Other: ___________

**Q2 — maps to Re-encounter (decomposition factor)**
How often do you actually go back and look at your wishlist after adding something?
- [ ] Same day
- [ ] Within a week
- [ ] Only when I get a notification or reminder
- [ ] Rarely
- [ ] Never — I forget about it

**Q3 — maps to H1 / the segment × barrier matrix (the single most important question)**
When you save something but don't buy it right away, what's usually the ONE biggest reason?
- [ ] Not sure it'll fit
- [ ] Not sure about the price (waiting for a drop, or comparing elsewhere)
- [ ] Not sure it suits the occasion I'd wear it for
- [ ] Not sure about the quality
- [ ] I forgot about it
- [ ] It went out of stock or my size disappeared
- [ ] Other: ___________

**Q4 — maps to H4 (resolution happens outside the app)**
Before deciding on a wishlisted item, do you look for information outside the app?
- [ ] Yes — I check Instagram/YouTube for reviews or try-on videos
- [ ] Yes — I ask friends or family
- [ ] Yes — I check other shopping sites
- [ ] No — I decide using only what's on the app

**Q5 — maps to Part 4's "existing workaround" requirement, and to kill criterion #4 in v2**
Have you ever ordered the SAME item in two different sizes on purpose, intending to return whichever one doesn't fit?
- [ ] Yes, regularly
- [ ] Yes, a few times
- [ ] No, never
- [ ] I didn't know people did this

**Q6 — maps to H3 (segment-level barrier differences)**
When you're unsure about an item, what would help you decide fastest?
- [ ] Seeing exactly how it compares to something I already own and kept
- [ ] Seeing real customer photos of people with different body types
- [ ] A clearer, faster size chart
- [ ] Knowing the price won't drop soon
- [ ] More reviews specifically about fit

**Q7 — maps to H5 (availability decay)**
Has an item you saved ever gone out of stock, or sold out in your size, before you decided to buy it?
- [ ] Yes, this happens often
- [ ] Yes, once or twice
- [ ] No, never
- [ ] Not sure

**Q8 — maps to MVP calibration/trust design (E8 guardrail thinking, gathered early)**
If an app told you "this size will fit you" based on your past orders, how much would you trust that?
- [ ] Fully trust it, would buy without checking further
- [ ] Trust it, but would still double-check the size chart
- [ ] Somewhat trust it, would want to know *why* it's saying that
- [ ] Wouldn't trust an app to know this

**Q9 — maps to the metric definition (sanity-checks the 30-day window against real behaviour)**
Roughly how many items are sitting in your wishlist right now that you added more than a month ago and still haven't bought?
- [ ] 0
- [ ] 1–3
- [ ] 4–10
- [ ] More than 10
- [ ] I don't check my wishlist count

**Q10 — segment marker, no dedicated hypothesis, used only to cross-tab Q3/Q6 by rough demographic**
Which best describes you right now?
- [ ] Student
- [ ] Working professional
- [ ] Other

### Optional open text (single field, not required)

**Q11 (optional).** In one sentence: what's stopped you from buying the last item still sitting in your wishlist?

---

### What was deliberately left out

No NPS-style satisfaction question, no "how would you rate the app" generic question, no demographic fields beyond Q10 (age/income bracketing adds analysis overhead without feeding a hypothesis), no multi-select "check all that apply" barrier question (forces a real priority ranking instead, which is what the kill criteria actually need). If a question doesn't trace to a row in `docs/hypotheses.md`, it doesn't belong in this form.
